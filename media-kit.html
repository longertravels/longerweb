On pricing
One person writes the script, films, edits, handles comms — and often pays their own travel. A brand hiring a traditional crew pays flights, accommodation and a production team on top. $200 for a minute of footage isn't a premium, it's a discount. Please don't ask me to undercut it.

How a collaboration works

  1. You send a brief
    Product, key message, platforms, any must-hit shots. If you don't have a brief, I'll write one for you.
  2. I send a concept + quote
    Shot list, script outline, delivery date and a fixed price — no surprises.
  3. Contract & 50% deposit
    Usage rights, duration, revision rounds and payment terms all in writing before I start.
  4. Filming & delivery
    Watermarked preview → your notes → final files on balance. Typically 7–10 days from brief to delivery.
